Online logbooks

Each logbook you set up gets an upload button next to Export ADIF. Nothing is sent until you click it, and QSOs logged without internet wait until you do. Already uploading with GridTracker? Then you can leave these empty.

On QRZ.com, open your logbook and press "Settings": the key is listed under API.

You're connected from another device. Online logbooks can only be set up on the computer running Procyon, but the upload buttons work from here.

Waterfall

Match your radio's receive filter so nothing is cut off at either edge, e.g. 300 to 3300 Hz. Your TX and RX offsets stay inside this range.

Quit Procyon?

Procyon stops listening and closes, along with its terminal window. Your settings and logbook are saved.
